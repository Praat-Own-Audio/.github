## First Look at Praat

Praat is for people who keep speech files they recorded and want one named praat desk, not mixed untitled sounds. It gives you own audio, a sound list, and a short path back to a spectrogram view you already keep. It does not replace the rest of your PC. It sits beside the files you already use.

Work stays on audio you recorded. Extra sounds wait for their own title.

## Inside Praat

Praat is a local Windows phonetics app with a named praat desk, a short sound list, and a clear last handoff. It does not try to replace the rest of the PC. It sits beside audio you already recorded and holds only the sound that is open now.
The layout stays small on purpose: one desk, one list, one path back. Extra sounds wait for their own title. Praat starts with a local profile and does not need a complex account system.
